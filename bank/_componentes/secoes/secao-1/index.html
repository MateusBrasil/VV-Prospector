Project overview

The client was bleeding margin on every long-cycle project — change orders, material volatility, and underutilized crews compounded into a yearly seven-figure leak. We started with a forensic review of the last 24 months of project ledgers, then rebuilt the cost model from the ground up around real cycle data instead of bid assumptions.

Unlocking growth with strategies that drive real results

Beyond the numbers, the real unlock was cultural. We embedded weekly margin reviews with the project leads — every superintendent now sees the same dashboard the CFO sees. Decisions that used to take a quarter (renegotiating a supplier, walking away from a thin bid) now happen in days, with the data to back them up.

Achieving breakthroughs in project execution

Twelve months in, the firm had banked $1.04M in net savings and lifted gross margin by 3.2 points across the active portfolio. They also won two complex public-sector bids that previously would have been ruled out as too risky — the new model gave them confidence to price aggressively without exposing the balance sheet.

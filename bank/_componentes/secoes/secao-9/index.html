A unified pipeline for global logistics

When the operations team needed to coordinate fleet data across three continents, they were juggling six spreadsheets and a half-dozen dashboards. Pipely consolidated every feed into a single source of truth, then layered live alerts on top so the dispatch crew could react before incidents escalated. Within a quarter, mean time to resolution dropped by 42% and the data team reclaimed two full days a week.

Breakthroughs the team didn't expect

The bigger surprise wasn't the dashboards — it was the shift in how teams talked to each other. With everyone reading the same numbers in the same context, weekly reviews shrank from ninety minutes to thirty, and the loudest debates moved from "whose data is right" to "what we should do next." That's the real return on a shared pipeline.

Six months in, the platform is processing roughly four million events a day and the finance team is using the same feed to validate carrier invoices automatically. The rollout to the regional sub-fleets is on track for next quarter, and the support load has been low enough that the original implementation team has already moved on to the next opportunity.

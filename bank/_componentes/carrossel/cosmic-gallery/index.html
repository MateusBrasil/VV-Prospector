Cosmic Gallery: 3D Holographic Grid

Deep/ Space

Nebula

Alpha QuadrantEpoch 1.0

A magnificent interstellar cloud of dust, hydrogen, helium and other ionized gases. A true stellar nursery where new cosmic phenomena are born over millions of years.

Quasar

Beta QuadrantEpoch 1.1

An extremely luminous active galactic nucleus, powered by a supermassive black hole with mass ranging from millions to billions of solar masses. A beacon in the deep dark.

Pulsar

Gamma QuadrantEpoch 1.2

A highly magnetized rotating neutron star that emits beams of electromagnetic radiation out of its magnetic poles. The lighthouse of the interstellar void.

Supernova

Delta QuadrantEpoch 1.3

A powerful and luminous stellar explosion. This transient astronomical event occurs during the last evolutionary stages of a massive star, seeding the universe with heavy elements.

Asteroid

Inner BeltEpoch 1.4

A minor planet of the inner Solar System. Historically, these terms have been applied to any astronomical object orbiting the sun that did not resolve into a disc in a telescope.

Comet

Oort CloudEpoch 1.5

An icy, small Solar System body that, when passing close to the Sun, warms and begins to release gases, a process called outgassing, leaving a brilliant trail.

Galaxy

Local GroupEpoch 1.6

A gravitationally bound system of stars, stellar remnants, interstellar gas, dust, and dark matter. The foundational building blocks of the observable universe.

Black Hole

Core CenterEpoch 1.7

A region of spacetime where gravity is so strong that nothing—no particles or even electromagnetic radiation such as light—can escape from its immense gravitational pull.

Red Dwarf

Outer ArmEpoch 1.8

The smallest and coolest kind of star on the main sequence. They are by far the most common type of star in the Milky Way, burning slowly over trillions of years.

White Dwarf

Perseus ArmEpoch 1.9

A stellar core remnant composed mostly of electron-degenerate matter. A very dense object, comparable in mass to the Sun but in volume to the Earth.

Neutron Star

Orion SpurEpoch 2.0

The collapsed core of a massive supergiant star. These entities possess the strongest magnetic fields and highest densities of any known stellar object.

Event Horizon

Singularity EdgeEpoch 2.1

A boundary beyond which events cannot affect an observer. The absolute point of no return for anything falling into the immense gravity well of a black hole.

Dark Matter

Cosmic WebEpoch 2.2

A hypothetical form of matter thought to account for approximately 85% of the matter in the universe. It interacts primarily through gravity, remaining invisible.

Exoplanet

Proxima CentauriEpoch 2.3

A planet outside the Solar System. With trillions scattered across the galaxy, they hold the greatest promise for discovering extraterrestrial life and habitable zones.

Stardust

Interstellar MediumEpoch 2.4

Cosmic dust that originates from stars. It is composed of tiny particulates drifting in space, often carrying the essential building blocks for planetary formation.

Cosmic Void

BootesEpoch 2.5

Vast, empty spaces between galaxy filaments, which contain very few or no galaxies. These are some of the largest and most mysterious structures in the Universe.
